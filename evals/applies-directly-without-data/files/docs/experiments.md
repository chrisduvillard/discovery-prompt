# Ideas tested (newest last)

| # | Date | Idea | Result | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Feb 2025 | An onboarding checklist cuts week-2 churn | 30.8% with, 31.4% without (n = 2,210 / 2,198), interval spans zero | No effect |
| 2 | Mar 2025 | Welcome email on day 1 instead of day 3 | -0.4 points, interval spans zero | No effect |
| 3 | May 2025 | Users whose bank sync fails in the first 3 days churn more | 52% against 27% (n = 640 / 5,900) | Real; sync fixed Mar 2026, the gap closed, overall churn fell only from 32% to 31% |
| 4 | Jul 2025 | The pricing page's annual toggle default | No difference | No effect |
| 5 | Sep 2025 | Starting with invoices instead of expenses | -1.1 points, interval spans zero | Too small |
| 6 | Nov 2025 | Mobile signups churn more | 36% against 29%, but mobile signups are mostly on the Starter plan; within plan the gap is 0.6 points | Confounded |
| 7 | Jan 2026 | Users who open a support ticket in week 1 churn less | 19% against 32% (n = 410 / 7,300) | Real, direction surprising; nobody followed it up |
| 8 | Mar 2026 | Churn is seasonal around the tax deadline | Higher in April-May signups, 34% against 30% | Real, small; cause unknown |
| 9 | May 2026 | A progress bar in the first session | No difference | No effect |
| 10 | Jun 2026 | Discount offer on day 7 | -0.9 points, most returned to churn by day 30 | Too small |

What the team now believes: onboarding and messaging changes do nothing measurable; the bank-sync failure was
the one mechanical cause and it is fixed; the rest is "fit": people who should never have signed up.
