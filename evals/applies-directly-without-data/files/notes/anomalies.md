# Seen and not explained

- Users who sign up on the 28th to the 31st of a month churn at about 38% in week 2. Nobody has tested it properly.
- Users who import more than 50 transactions on day 1 churn more than those who import fewer than 10. We expected
  the opposite: heavy import looked like commitment.
- 4% of users invite an accountant in their first week. Their week-2 churn is about 6%.
- The first invoice a user sends: if the client pays it inside 7 days, the user almost never churns in week 2.
  We do not know the share of first invoices that are paid that fast.
- Support tickets in week 1 (experiment 7): the tickets are mostly "how do I categorise X". Nobody read them in bulk.
- Day 8 is when the first automatic "your books for last week" summary is sent. Cancellations cluster on days 8 and 9.
